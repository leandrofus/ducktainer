# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone who takes part in ducktainer, in issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces, to have a respectful and harassment-free experience, whatever their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps build a welcoming project includes:

- being respectful and constructive, and assuming good intent;
- accepting feedback on code and ideas, and giving it clearly;
- acknowledging when someone else's work or reasoning is what made a change better;
- focusing on what is best for users and the project.

Behavior that is not acceptable includes:

- harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling;
- publishing others' private information without their permission;
- sexualized language or imagery, and unwanted attention;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability**), or to the maintainer through GitHub. Reports are reviewed and handled confidentially. The maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may block people who repeatedly break it.
